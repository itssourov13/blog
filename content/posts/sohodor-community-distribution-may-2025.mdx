On **30 May 2025**, we carried out a distribution as part of the Sohodor community effort. It was the practical next step after contributions had been brought together and the items had been prepared for handover.

A distribution is more than the moment an item changes hands. It takes coordination, preparation, and people willing to give their time. What matters most is that the support reaches someone in a useful and respectful way.

## Moments from the distribution

These photographs preserve a few moments connected with the handover. They show prepared packages and recipients receiving items; they are only a small part of the wider effort.

## A small record worth keeping

I have not added a recipient count, item-by-item inventory, or location because those details are not available in my notes. The date of the distribution, however, is clear: **30 May 2025**.

I am keeping this post as a record of the collective effort behind the day: the contributions, the preparation, and the act of sharing. I am grateful to everyone who helped make it happen.
