Community work often begins long before the moment people see in a photograph. In May 2025, I joined an effort to collect contributions for **Sohodor**, bringing support together for a planned distribution.

The idea was straightforward: individual contributions, when gathered with care, could be turned into practical support for people who would receive the distributed items. The fundraising was one part of a wider effort that also involved preparing the packages and arranging the handover.

## A note about the date and the record

I no longer have the exact date on which the contributions were collected. To keep this memory in the right place in the archive, I have used **28 May 2025** as an approximate date, ahead of the distribution on 30 May 2025. It is an estimate, not a confirmed collection date.

I have also left out a fundraising total, donor count, and other figures that I cannot reliably verify. Recording a community effort should not mean filling gaps in the record with guesses.

## What I want to remember

The value of a community effort is not measured only by a number. It also lives in the people who contribute, the time spent preparing, and the coordination that turns an intention into something practical.

This post is a small record of the fundraising side of the Sohodor effort. The next part of the story is the distribution itself, which took place on **30 May 2025**.
